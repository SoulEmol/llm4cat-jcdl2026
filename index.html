About the tutorial

Turn emerging AI capabilities into responsible collection workflows.

Digital libraries, museums, and cultural heritage institutions manage growing collections of text, images, and multimodal materials. Organizing these resources remains labor-intensive, while metadata backlogs and inconsistent description can limit discovery.

This tutorial connects core model concepts with practical applications. Through accessible explanations, demonstrations, and guided exercises, participants will use LLMs for textual metadata tasks and VLMs for archival image description—then examine how to evaluate, refine, and responsibly integrate their outputs.
